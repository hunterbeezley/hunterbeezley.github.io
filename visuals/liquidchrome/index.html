Liquid Chrome

2024 TouchDesigner

About This Project

Liquid Chrome is an exploration of fluid dynamics and reflection. The piece simulates metallic liquid surfaces with physically-inspired fluid motion, creating a mesmerizing interaction between form and light. The work focuses on how reflections and refractions transform simple geometric surfaces into complex, organic-feeling forms.

Technical Approach

This project leverages TouchDesigner's GPU-accelerated fluid solver to simulate liquid behavior in real-time. Custom shaders handle reflections, refractions, and metallic surface properties. The result is a smooth, continuous animation that suggests liquid movement while maintaining visual clarity.

The work demonstrates advanced shader techniques and optimization strategies for rendering complex materials in real-time contexts.
