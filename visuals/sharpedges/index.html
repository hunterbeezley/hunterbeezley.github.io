Sharp Edges

2024 TouchDesigner, Procedural Generation

About This Project

Sharp Edges is a study in angular geometry and crystalline structures. The piece uses procedural algorithms to generate complex, interlocking geometric forms that evolve and transform over time. By focusing on hard edges and precise angles, the work creates tension and visual interest through the interplay of light, shadow, and mathematical precision.

Technical Approach

Built using TouchDesigner's procedural generation capabilities combined with custom GLSL shaders, Sharp Edges demonstrates algorithmic geometry at scale. The system generates crystalline structures using noise functions and geometric primitives, then applies complex lighting models to create depth and visual complexity.

The procedural approach allows for infinite variation while maintaining visual coherence through underlying mathematical rules.
